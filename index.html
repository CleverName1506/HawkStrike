WHAT TO REMEMBER

Keep app instructions and visitor input separate.

A database should receive its fixed instruction separately from the value a person submitted. This is the central idea behind parameterized queries.

ONE INPUT, THREE OUTCOMES

Why a quick patch is not enough.

RISKY

Mixes the two

App instructions and visitor input become one piece of text.

QUICK PATCH

Tries to block clues

Filtering suspicious-looking text can miss variations and does not fix the underlying design.

SECURE

Uses separate lanes

The app's instruction is fixed; the visitor value stays data.
